Editor's Speech

Welcome to Daily News Portal. Our mission is to deliver unbiased, accurate, and timely news to readers across the nation. For over fifteen years, we have upheld the highest standards of journalism, believing that an informed public is the foundation of a strong democracy.

We are committed to investigative reporting, ethical practices, and giving voice to stories that matter. Thank you for trusting us as your news source.

— Ram Sharma, Chief Editor

Advertisement Policy

Daily News Portal maintains clear guidelines for all advertising content displayed on our platform:

  • All advertisements must be clearly labeled and distinguishable from editorial content.
  • We do not accept advertisements for illegal products, misleading claims, or harmful substances.
  • Political advertisements must comply with election commission regulations and include proper disclaimers.
  • Advertisers must provide accurate contact information and abide by contract terms.
  • The editorial team reserves the right to reject any advertisement that conflicts with our values.

For advertising inquiries, contact ads@dailyportal.com.

News Policy

Our news policy ensures integrity and accountability in every story we publish:

  • Accuracy: All facts are verified through at least two independent sources before publication.
  • Impartiality: We present balanced coverage without favoring any political party or interest group.
  • Corrections: Errors are corrected promptly with visible updates on affected articles.
  • Privacy: We respect individual privacy and protect confidential sources.
  • Attribution: Quotes, data, and images are properly credited to their sources.
